# Why I Created DaysFromToday

Hello, I'm Leon.

I currently live in London, working as an internet product manager and an independent investor.

Time — for each of us, it's almost the only **fair, scarce, and completely controllable resource** worth mastering.

<font color="#245bdb">By accurately grasping time, we can regain control over our lives.</font>

---

In September this year, my 13-year-old son began his first full boarding school life.
He entered a secondary school located in the suburbs of London.

This brought enormous changes to his life, because for the past ten-plus years, we had seen each other almost every day.
From now on, he can only come home once every 3-4 weeks.

Actually, this is not just a challenge for him, but also for his mother and me.

---

A few days after he started boarding school, we agreed to call each other every day.

Almost every time we talk, he mentions one word — **time**.

> "Dad, there are only 3 days left until the weekend, then I won't have to get up at 6:30 AM!"
>
>
> "Dad, there are only 3 weeks left until we can see each other, I can come home for good food!"
>
>
> "Dad, how many months until my birthday?"

Every time he asks these questions, I would frantically open my calendar while on the phone to help him calculate an exact date.
He loves hearing "countdowns,"
as if with each day less, happiness gets a little closer.

I know he has a bit of "time anxiety."

But I think this is actually a good thing —
a boy learning to master his own time resources.

---

Actually, for adults, we also do "time calculations" every day.

> How long has it been since the last loan payment? How many more years until my debt is cleared?
>
>
> How long until my wife and I's wedding anniversary? I can't forget again.
>
>
> When is the family gathering? How many days left? Need to prepare in advance, don't be as rushed as last year.

But in various calendar applications, **no one helps us calculate "countdowns."**

---

So I created this application — **Days From Today**.

It was initially just to conveniently answer my son's questions,
and to remind me of those important dates and anniversaries.

Later I thought, maybe there are many fathers like me in the world,
or people who also hope to control their own time.

---

**<font color="#245bdb">If you also hope to regain control of your time,</font>**
**<font color="#245bdb">I hope _Days From Today_ can help you a little bit.</font>**

Thank you for reading this far.

I wish you a pleasant, free, and time-controlled life.

If you have any thoughts or feelings,

I sincerely welcome you to tell me.

Thank you.

— Leon
