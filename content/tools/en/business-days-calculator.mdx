# Business Days Calculator: A Complete Guide

Understanding business days is crucial for project planning, legal deadlines, and financial calculations.

## What Are Business Days?

Business days typically refer to weekdays (Monday through Friday) excluding public holidays.

### Key Characteristics

- **Weekdays Only**: Monday through Friday
- **Excludes Holidays**: Public and bank holidays
- **Regional Variations**: Different countries have different holiday calendars

## How to Calculate Business Days

### Basic Calculation

1. **Start Date**: The beginning of your calculation period
2. **End Date**: The end of your calculation period
3. **Exclude Weekends**: Skip Saturday and Sunday
4. **Exclude Holidays**: Remove public holidays from the count

### Example Calculation

If you start a project on Monday, January 6th and need 5 business days:

- Monday, Jan 6 (Day 1)
- Tuesday, Jan 7 (Day 2)
- Wednesday, Jan 8 (Day 3)
- Thursday, Jan 9 (Day 4)
- Friday, Jan 10 (Day 5)

The project would be completed on Friday, January 10th.
