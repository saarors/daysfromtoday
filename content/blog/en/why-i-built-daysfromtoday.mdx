# Why I Built DaysFromToday

In September, my 13-year-old son started boarding school. Every call, he asks: "How many days left?" It made me realize — time is the only truly fair and scarce resource we have.

## The Realization

When my son first went to boarding school, I didn't think much about the days. But then I started counting. Not just the days until his next visit home, but the days until holidays, the days until summer break, the days until he graduates.

Each day became precious. Each day became a countdown to something important.

## Why Days Matter

Time is the only resource that's truly equal for everyone. We all get 24 hours in a day, 365 days in a year. But how we use that time, how we count that time, makes all the difference.

- **Days until a deadline** - creates urgency
- **Days until a vacation** - builds anticipation  
- **Days until a milestone** - measures progress
- **Days until a reunion** - counts down to joy

## The Problem I Wanted to Solve

I searched for a simple tool to calculate days from today. Most were cluttered, slow, or didn't work properly. I wanted something that:

- **Just works** - no complicated setup
- **Is fast** - instant results
- **Handles business days** - excludes weekends and holidays
- **Supports multiple countries** - because holidays vary by location
- **Works in multiple languages** - because time is universal

## Building DaysFromToday

So I built DaysFromToday. A simple, fast, powerful tool that does one thing really well: calculate dates from today.

### Key Features

- **Natural Days**: Count all days including weekends
- **Business Days**: Exclude weekends and holidays
- **Multiple Countries**: Support for 15+ countries
- **Multiple Languages**: English and Chinese
- **Anniversary Tracking**: Remember important dates
- **Mobile Friendly**: Works on any device

## The Philosophy

DaysFromToday isn't just a date calculator. It's a tool for:

- **Planning** - know exactly when things will happen
- **Anticipation** - count down to exciting events
- **Remembrance** - track anniversaries and milestones
- **Productivity** - plan around business days and holidays

## The Future

Every day, I think about my son asking "How many days left?" And I'm reminded why I built this tool. Not just for him, but for everyone who needs to count days.

Because when you know exactly how many days are left, you can make the most of every single one.

---

*This is why I built DaysFromToday. For my son, for my family, and for everyone who understands that time is the most precious resource we have.*
